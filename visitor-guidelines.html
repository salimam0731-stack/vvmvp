Visitor Information

GENERAL GUIDELINES
  • Maintain silence in meditation halls and other designated areas on campus.
  • Park your vehicle only in designated areas. Our security team will be available to guide and assist you with parking.
  • Shuttle service is available inside the ashram for senior citizens and those with walking difficulty. Wheelchairs can be requested from the Information Center for those who need them.

  • We encourage visitors to walk around Campus and soak in its natural surroundings.
  • Kindly use headphones while listening to music or playing any audio on your mobile phones.
  • As this is a sacred and culturally significant place, we kindly request guests to dress modestly and respectfully. Indian ethnic wear is preferred, with comfortable clothing covering the shoulders and knees. Please avoid sleeveless, very short, transparent, crop tops, or excessively tight/body-revealing outfits.
  • Please remove your footwear before entering halls, temples and meditation spaces.
  • Our Annapoorna Dining Facility serves wholesome vegetarian food only. Non vegetarian food is strictly prohibited on Campus.
  • Art of Living International Center, Bengaluru is an intoxication-free Campus. We strictly prohibit the consumption of liquor, tobacco, cigarettes, and hallucinogens within the premises and its vicinity.
  • Please use the dustbins and help us keep the Campus clean and green.
  • Kindly avoid public display of affection (PDA) on Campus.
  • Kindly take care of your belongings. Ashram authority will not be responsible for any loss.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times for their safety.
  • Availability of Campus Tour can be checked with Information Center upon arrival.
  • Please do not feed the birds and animals on Campus. They are happiest finding their own food.
  • Campus is home to diverse flora and fauna. Visitors are requested not to disturb or provoke them.
  • Please do not pluck flowers. Pluck them with your eyes, not your hands, so everyone can enjoy them.
SATSANG GUIDELINES
  • Satsang timings: Monday - Saturday: 6:30 - 8 PM IST | Sunday: 6 - 8 PM IST
  • Once Gurudev arrives for Satsang please remain seated and avoid unnecessary movement.
  • Please maintain silence / talk softly during Satsang.
  • Photography and Videography is prohibited.
  • Footwear is strictly not permitted inside Dhyan Mandir and VM Ground Floor (depending on where the satsang is taking place). Please remove your shoes / slippers before entering.
  • Kindly avoid walking or moving around during the Satsang.
  • Kindly do not change the arrangement or position of the chairs inside the hall.
  • Kindly ensure your mobile phone is on silent mode or switched off during Satsang.
  • Separate seating areas are designated for male and female participants. Kindly ensure that you are seated in the respective area.
  • We request parents to ensure children don't run around or play during Satsang, or to be seated in the area assigned for parents with kids.
