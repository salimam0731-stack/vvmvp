About Ved Vignan Maha Vidya Peeth

Established in 1981, Ved Vignan Maha Vidya Peeth (VVMVP) is a public charitable trust under the aegis of The Art of Living. We are dedicated to transforming lives through education, empowerment, and enlightenment.

Education Excellence

We provide underprivileged children with comprehensive free education, transportation, uniforms, books, and nutritious mid-day meals.

Women Empowerment

Our skill development programs help women build self-reliance and achieve economic independence.

Rural Development

We promote sustainable practices and help build essential infrastructure in rural communities.

Vedic Research

We are committed to studying, preserving, and sharing the knowledge and wisdom of the Vedic tradition.

Medical & Social Research

We advance healthcare initiatives and social welfare programs for the betterment of communities.
