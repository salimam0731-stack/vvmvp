About Ved Vignan Maha Vidya Peeth

Ved Vignan Maha Vidya Peeth (VVMVP) is a distinguished public charitable trust established in 1981 under the aegis of The Art of Living, dedicated to transforming lives through education, empowerment, and enlightenment.

Education Excellence

Providing comprehensive free education, transportation, uniforms, books, and nutritious mid-day meals to underprivileged children.

Women Empowerment

Implementing skill development programs that foster self-reliance and economic independence among women.

Rural Development

Promoting sustainable practices and building essential infrastructure in rural communities.

Vedic Research

Committed to the study, preservation, and propagation of ancient Vedic knowledge and wisdom.

Medical & Social Research

Advancing healthcare initiatives and social welfare programs for community betterment.
